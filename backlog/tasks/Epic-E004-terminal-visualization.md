# Epic E004 — Terminal Visualization

## Objective
Render the synthesis process and resulting circuit in the terminal.

## Scope
Display rational functions, state, options, component values, removal sequence, and consistent ASCII diagrams.

## Tasks

- Task E004.001 — Render synthesis information
- Task E004.002 — Render ASCII circuit diagrams

## Completion Criteria

Intermediate and final output is readable in the terminal and uses consistent labels for components, terminals, connections, and ground.
