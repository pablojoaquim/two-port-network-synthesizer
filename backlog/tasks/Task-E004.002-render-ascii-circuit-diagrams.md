# Task E004.002 — Render ASCII Circuit Diagrams

## Objective
Render intermediate and final circuit representations using consistent ASCII notation.

## Context
The application presents synthesis steps and the final circuit graphically in the terminal.

## Requirements

- Render intermediate synthesis diagrams.
- Render the final circuit representation.
- Use consistent labels for components, terminals, connections, and ground.

## Design References

- README.md: Terminal Visualization and Final Output
- docs/sdd.md: Sections 3, 5, and 8

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] An intermediate circuit state can be rendered as ASCII.
- [ ] A final circuit can be rendered as ASCII.
- [ ] Labels and connection notation are consistent between intermediate and final output.

## Tests

Test rendering of representative intermediate and final circuit states.

## Definition of Done

ASCII rendering and focused output tests are implemented and pass.
